About Refrigeration Knowledge

Refrigeration Knowledge is an open technical educational resource dedicated to explaining thermodynamic principles, component mechanics, and fluid dynamics in vapour-compression refrigeration systems.

Target Audience & Scope

This documentation is designed specifically for:

  • HVAC-R apprentices, certificate trainees, and technical trade students.
  • Mechanical and building services engineering students studying thermodynamics.
  • Facility managers and equipment operators seeking a deeper physical understanding of cooling equipment.

Authoritative Source Foundations

All thermodynamic definitions, formulas, and performance data are aligned with internationally recognised engineering authorities:

  • American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers (ASHRAE Handbooks).
  • Australian Institute of Refrigeration, Air Conditioning and Heating (AIRAH).
  • Australian Refrigeration Council (ARCTick) licensing framework.
  • Standard international thermodynamic property formulations (IAPWS, NIST Refprop).

Network Relationship Disclosures

Refrigeration Knowledge operates as the theoretical and technical branch of an integrated Australian refrigeration publication network:

  • Fridge Care Guide (Cloudflare Pages): Domestic consumer refrigerator care and DIY troubleshooting.
  • Commercial Refrigeration Guide (Vercel): Food service kitchen compliance and coolroom HACCP logging.
  • Sydney Refrigeration Resource (Netlify): The central regional pillar resource connecting practical maintenance with licensed Sydney trade services.

All technical content is provided without commercial bias or manufactured case studies. Read our Editorial Policy & Standards.

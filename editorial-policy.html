Editorial & Engineering Documentation Policy

Standards of thermodynamic fact-checking, safety limitations, and versioned technical accuracy.

1. Scientific & Engineering Rigor

Every formula, pressure-enthalpy curve, and thermodynamic relationship presented in this documentation conforms to standard SI metric units (kPa, kJ/kg, K) and established physical laws of thermodynamics (Boyle-Charles, Fourier conduction, First & Second Laws).

2. Safety & Regulated Trade Restrictions

This documentation is educational and theoretical in nature. It does not constitute step-by-step DIY field repair instructions.

Under Australian Commonwealth law (Ozone Protection and Synthetic Greenhouse Gas Management Act 1989), accessing sealed refrigerant circuits, recovering synthetic fluorocarbons, brazing pressurized tubing, or commissioning refrigeration equipment requires an appropriately authorized technician holding a valid ARCTick Refrigerant Handling Licence. We explicitly discourage unlicensed individuals from attempting hazardous high-pressure refrigerant work.

3. Revisions & Technical Corrections

We continually maintain this documentation tree. When equipment technologies evolve (such as transitioning from high-GWP HFCs to ultra-low-GWP natural refrigerants like R290 and CO2), articles are updated and versioned.

To propose technical errata, suggest improvements, or report formula typos, contact our team via our Technical Contact Page.

4. Institutional & Network Independence

Refrigeration Knowledge does not sell refrigeration hardware or accept paid vendor product placement. We maintain cross-property references to our sister publications (Fridge Care Guide, Commercial Refrigeration Guide, and the Sydney Refrigeration Resource) solely to provide readers with contextual domestic, commercial, and regional repair information.
